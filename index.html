Travel tips before you fly

  1. Keep your passport and documents safe, with digital copies too.
  2. Check the local weather before you pack.
  3. Carry essential medicines in your hand luggage.
  4. Respect local culture, dress codes and customs.
  5. Keep emergency contacts saved and written down.
